## 2026-05-19 - Dynamic ARIA labels for stateful toggle buttons
**Learning:** Adding dynamic `aria-label` and `title` attributes to stateful toggle buttons (like the sidebar toggle) improves accessibility for screen reader users and provides better context for all users.
**Action:** When implementing interactive toggle buttons, ensure their `aria-label` and `title` explicitly reflect the action that will happen when clicked, depending on the current state.
