## 2024-06-12 - Added ARIA labels to structural layout components
**Learning:** Structural layout components like the Top Bar and Sidebar heavily use icon-only buttons (`<Button size="icon">`) for critical navigation paths that lack accessible names, breaking screen reader navigation.
**Action:** Always verify that interactive icon-only elements include an explicit `aria-label` describing their function.
